## Search Logic

The system will monitor new papers and prioritize papers related to:

- high entropy
- entropy engineering
- high-entropy sulfide
- sulfide anode
- metal sulfide
- sodium-ion battery
- Na-ion battery
- sodium storage
- conversion reaction
- alloying reaction
- solid-state electrolyte
